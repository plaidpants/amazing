"Amazing"

(c) 1984 Steve Capps

Decompiled from the original 68000 APPL/MAZE binary on Amazing.dsk.

Two Independent Maze Planes (DRAWSQUA): Skill 1 is a single wide maze. Skill 2 adds 90° wide overpasses. Skill 3 adds a completely separate Narrow Upper Maze right on top of the wide maze (since narrow paths are 6px wide, the 14px wide paths remain visible underneath!).

4 Stacked Paths (ISBRIDGE + ISRAMP): Both the wide maze below and the narrow maze above can cross themselves at 90° with overpasses—allowing up to 4 paths in a single square—connected between planes by 45° diagonal ramps!

Start & Goal Markers (MARKENDS): Navigate from the solid circle marker (●) to the solid square marker (■)!
